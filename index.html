Irisgrain

Film recipes, pushed to your camera

Keep your film-look recipes in one place and send them to your camera over a USB cable, instead of dialling in settings menu by menu.

Coming soon to Google Play

Push in one tap

Every setting is read back after a push, so you see exactly what applied. Track the camera's custom slots too.

A library that fits

Paste recipe text from any page. Each recipe shows what fits your camera, what's adapted and why.

Recipe from an image

Pick a photo with a look you love and get the nearest camera settings, with a reason for each.

See it first

Preview a look live over your phone's camera, or export it as a .cube LUT for your editor.

Frame and shoot

Composition guides, a level, lens matching and photo sessions with a review page.

Camera scanner

Scan any USB camera for the settings it exposes and send the profile to help add it.

What you need

A Fujifilm X-T1 in USB tether mode, and an Android phone with USB OTG and a cable to suit. More cameras are on the way: scanning yours and sending its profile helps.
